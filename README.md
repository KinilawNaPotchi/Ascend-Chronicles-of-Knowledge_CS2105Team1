# ✦ CHRONICLES OF KNOWLEDGE
### A Turn-Based Educational Adventure Game

*Recover your memories. Challenge your knowledge. Uncover the truth.*

---

## About the Game

**Chronicles of Knowledge** is a Java-based, turn-based educational adventure game that combines academic challenges with fantasy-inspired storytelling and RPG mechanics.

Players take on the role of a mysterious being who has lost their memories after a powerful entity shattered them into three fragments. Scattered across different dimensions, these fragments are guarded by powerful beings who will challenge the player's knowledge in Mathematics, Science, and Programming.

Through a series of question-based battles, players must defeat each guardian, recover their lost memories, and uncover the truth behind their forgotten identity.

But there is more to this journey than meets the eye. Every recovered fragment brings the player one step closer to a mystery that may be much bigger than the universe itself.

**Do you have what it takes to reclaim what was lost?**

---

## Game Features

- **Interactive Storyline** – Experience an original fantasy-inspired narrative through dialogue boxes and cutscenes.
- **Three Unique Dimensions** – Explore Mathematics, Science, and Programming, each with its own visual theme and guardian.
- **Question-Based Battles** – Test your knowledge through multiple-choice and typed-answer questions.
- **Turn-Based Combat** – Take turns challenging opponents through academic questions and battle actions.
- **Progressive Difficulty** – Face increasingly challenging questions and stronger guardians as you advance.
- **Ultimate Moves** – Unleash powerful attacks when a combatant reaches the critical 5 HP threshold.
- **Memory Collection** – Defeat the guardians and retrieve the fragments needed to restore your identity.
- **Character Dialogue** – Discover the personalities and stories of the characters through in-game interactions.

##  The Three Dimensions

Each dimension presents a different academic challenge, environment, and guardian.

### 1. Mathematics Dimension
**Theme:** Cosmic / Universe

A mysterious dimension inspired by the vastness of the universe, filled with celestial bodies, constellations, and cosmic energy.

- **Guardian:** Infinity
- **Weapon:** Star
- **Challenge:** Mathematics questions

Prove your logical thinking and problem-solving abilities against a guardian who embodies the concept of infinity.

### 2. Science Dimension
**Theme:** Futuristic / Robotics

A technologically advanced dimension characterized by sleek white environments, futuristic laboratories, and sophisticated machinery.

- **Guardian:** The Automaton
- **Weapon:** Missile
- **Challenge:** Science questions

Put your understanding of scientific concepts to the test against a highly advanced robotic guardian.

### 3. Programming Dimension
**Theme:** Artificial Intelligence / Digital World

A digital dimension inspired by modern AI assistants, featuring corrupted interfaces, virtual environments, and unexpected system errors.

- **Guardian:** The Glitch
- **Weapon:** Error
- **Challenge:** Programming questions

Navigate the digital chaos and challenge an AI-inspired guardian whose responses are not always as helpful as they seem.

##  Gameplay

**Chronicles of Knowledge** uses a turn-based, question-driven battle system.

During battles:

1. A question appears in the designated question panel.
2. The player answers through multiple-choice options or a typed response, depending on the question.
3. The battle progresses based on the player's answer.
4. Opponents present their own challenges as the battle continues.
5. Both the player and the opponent have health points (HP) that determine the battle's outcome.

### Ultimate Moves

When either the player or the boss reaches **5 HP**, an ultimate move can be unleashed.

These special attacks add an exciting twist to battles and can become a decisive moment in the fight.

### Difficulty Progression

As players advance through the dimensions, the challenges become increasingly difficult. Each guardian presents a greater test of knowledge and strategy.

##  Storyline

Once, there existed a being with extraordinary intelligence and knowledge beyond the comprehension of ordinary life.

However, an entity known as **The Shatterer** feared the consequences of allowing a single being to possess such immense knowledge.

To preserve what it believed to be the balance of the universe, the entity shattered the being's memories into three fragments and scattered them across different dimensions.

Stripped of their memories and physical form, the being awakens as a spirit in the Consciousness Realm.

With the guidance of a mysterious guardian, the player must travel through the three dimensions, overcome their guardians, and recover the fragments of their forgotten past.

But as the memories return, a greater mystery begins to unfold.

**Is reclaiming one's memories truly the end of the journey?**

##  Educational Objectives

The game aims to make learning more engaging through interactive gameplay and knowledge-based challenges.

It focuses on three academic areas:

| Dimension | Academic Coverage |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | Mathematical concepts, calculations, logic, and problem-solving |
| Science | Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and scientific concepts |
| Programming | Programming fundamentals, syntax, Object-Oriented Programming, Git, and Data Structures and Algorithms |

By integrating academic questions into an adventure-style game, players can practice recalling concepts, applying knowledge, and solving problems in an interactive environment.
